Lab 11: Ethical Hacking Part 1

Challenges: This lab was mostly self-guided, which made it feel more like a real-world challenge. I didn’t have step-by-step instructions, so I had to rely on my knowledge to explore the files and find weaknesses. It was difficult figuring out where to start and what tools would help. I had to be careful not to overlook small details that could lead to the flag. It was one of the most open ended labs we’ve done. Process & Tools: I analyzed files and system output that simulated a vulnerable environment. I looked for patterns, clues, and hidden content that would reveal the next steps. I applied different ethical hacking techniques to uncover weaknesses and tested commands cautiously. I kept notes on what worked and what didn’t and documented my findings as if reporting them responsibly. This helped me build a hacker’s mindset. Takeaways: This lab helped me understand how ethical hackers think and work through problems. It taught me to be persistent, creative, and cautious. I realized that not all vulnerabilities are obvious and that sometimes you have to dig deep. It was a good test of everything I’ve learned so far and showed me how to approach real challenges ethically and methodically.
