Lab 7: Implementing an IT Security Policy

Challenges: Applying security policies across multiple systems was more complex than I expected. Some settings didn’t stick right away and needed extra troubleshooting. I had to make sure everything from password rules to antivirus and mobile device security was correctly enforced. Getting policies to apply to mobile devices also required looking at settings I wasn’t as familiar with. It took patience to double-check that every rule was active and doing what it should. Process & Tools: I configured password and antivirus policies using Active Directory, then used Microsoft’s baseline settings to tighten security. I also applied settings on a mobile device, enabling encryption, screen locks, and app scanning. I tested features like Find My Device and reviewed policy enforcement logs. I researched acceptable use and privacy policies from real organizations to better understand how rules are created and applied. This gave me a good balance of technical skills and policy knowledge. Takeaways: This lab taught me that policies are just as important as tools when it comes to securing systems. I learned how to create rules that protect both devices and data. It also helped me understand that policies must be clear, enforceable, and tested regularly. Seeing the connection between mobile and desktop security made me appreciate the importance of consistency.
