Lab 6: Assessing Common Attack Vectors

Challenges: This lab involved a lot of different types of attacks, and it wasn’t always clear if they were working right away. Sometimes I had to reset the setup or rerun scripts to get proper results. It was a challenge to remember the correct steps for each attack type. I also had to make sure I didn’t crash the system while simulating a DoS attack. It really pushed me to work carefully and think ahead. Process & Tools: I performed SQL injections, created malware payloads with msfvenom, launched DoS attacks, and built phishing emails. I also tested some of the attacks against live systems in a lab setting. After each attack, I analyzed how it worked and what could have prevented it. Then I looked into defensive tools like input validation, antivirus software, and employee training. I made notes on how each attack type could be detected or stopped. Takeaways: I saw how easy it can be for attackers to exploit weak systems and how important it is to train employees and use strong defenses. This lab helped me understand how common attacks work from the inside. I learned how attackers think and how to defend against that mindset. It also made me more aware of how one vulnerability can lead to major issues.
