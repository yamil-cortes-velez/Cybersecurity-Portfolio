Lab 9: Configuring Backup and Recovery Functions

Challenges: Setting up backup schedules and testing failover functionality was a little overwhelming at first. I wasn’t sure how to simulate a failure or what to look for in the results. Getting both servers to load balance correctly took a few tries. I had to learn how to monitor traffic shifts and make sure everything was running smoothly. It was a good challenge that showed me how redundancy works in practice. Process & Tools: I used Windows Server Backup to create system state backups and schedule them at regular intervals. Then I configured HAProxy to distribute traffic between two servers and tested failover by taking one server offline. I verified the behavior by checking session logs and DNS responses. I also created NFS shares and confirmed data could still be accessed during a failure. The tools gave me a hands-on view of high availability setups. Takeaways: This lab helped me understand how backups and redundancy protect against data loss and downtime. It showed me that high availability isn’t just about having two servers, it’s about making sure they work together correctly. I gained practical experience in testing failovers and reading performance metrics. It made me feel more confident in my ability to plan for system recovery.
